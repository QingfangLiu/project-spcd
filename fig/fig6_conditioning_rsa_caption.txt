Figure 6. Lateral OFC represents reward identity during conditioning.

(A) Odor expectation during conditioning. Dashed branches link conditioned cues to their expected odors, shown in dashed boxes; solid boxes indicate delivered odors. Odor images are illustrative and were not displayed during the task. Odor delivery was modeled separately in the single-trial GLMs. (B) Odor-identity RSA. Spearman correlations between neural and expected odor-identity RDMs were computed from cue and prediction-period estimates (0-5 s; EXT5s), using correct food-odor trials across all available conditioning runs and restricting comparisons to different runs. Analyses used the same bilateral OFC regions as Figure 3. Points represent individual participants (n = 29), and open diamonds indicate means with t-based 95% confidence intervals. The dashed line indicates zero RSA correlation. Asterisks denote uncorrected p < 0.05 from one-sided one-sample t tests against zero; n.s., not significant.
