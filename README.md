# SPCD Task Overview  
**Sensory Preconditioning and Devaluation**

## Summary  
The SPCD task is a multi-day fMRI-compatible behavioral paradigm designed to study outcome devaluation and sensory preconditioning. It involves multiple phases including cue conditioning, odor-based associations, value updating via devaluation, and recognition memory testing. Participants complete tasks both inside and outside the MRI scanner.

---

## Day-by-Day Procedure

### Day 0 – Baseline Rating & Odor Selection
- **Image Rating Task**  
  Participants rate character and place images for later use in the task.
- **Initial Odor Rating & Selection**  
  Participants rate odor pleasantness and intensity. Two odors (one savory, one sweet) are selected for the experiment based on these ratings.
- **Follow-Up Odor Rating**  
  A repeat of the rating task to ensure consistency and finalize odor selection.
- **Odor Discrimination Task**  
  Judgment of whether two odors are the same or different. 

---

### Day 1 – Pre-conditioning & Localizer (fMRI)
- **Localizer Task**  
  Run only in the MRI scanner to obtain neural responses to individual visual images and odors, before any learning involved.
- **Pre-conditioning Task (outside the scanner)**  
  - Participants learn associations between characters and places.
  - Completed over **3–5 runs** (up to 5 max; task may end at the end of 3rd or 4th run if **≥90% accuracy** is achieved).

---

### Day 2 – Conditioning, Probe, Devaluation
- **Pre-conditioning Refresher (outside the scanner)**  
  - A brief refresher session, **1–3 runs** (up to 3 max; may end after 1 run if **≥90% accuracy**).
- **Odor Review**  
  - Quick exposure to odors for reminder and equipment testing purposes (**1 trial each**).
- **Odor Prediction Task**  
  - Participants predict which odor will follow a conditioned cue.  
  - **3 runs**, **48 trials per run**.  
  - Conducted in the scanner.
- **Pre-meal Probe**  
  - Tests responses to **pre-conditioned and conditioned cues** without odor delivery.  
  - **3 runs**, **32 trials per run**.
- **Meal / Devaluation Manipulation**  
  - Participants eat a meal with flavor matched to one of the two odors.  
  - **Pre- and Post-meal Odor Rating** to assess devaluation effect.
- **Post-meal Probe**  
  - Same as Probe 1 but occurs after devaluation.  
  - **3 runs**, **32 trials per run**.
- **Recognition Memory Task (outside the scanner)**  
  - Participants judge whether they have seen specific character-place pairs before. 
  - **1 run**, **48 trials**.
