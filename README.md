# SPCD Task Overview  
**Sensory Preconditioning and Devaluation**

## Summary  
The SPCD task is a multi-day fMRI-compatible behavioral paradigm designed to study outcome devaluation and sensory preconditioning. It involves multiple phases including cue conditioning, odor-based associations, value updating via devaluation, and recognition memory testing. Participants complete tasks both inside and outside the MRI scanner.

---

## Day-by-Day Procedure

### Day 0 – Baseline Rating & Odor Selection
- **Image Rating Task**  
  Participants rate character and place images for later use in the task.
- **Initial Odor Rating & Selection**  
  Participants rate odor pleasantness and intensity. A subset of odors is selected for the experiment based on these ratings.
- **Follow-Up Odor Rating**  
  A repeat of the rating task to ensure consistency and finalize odor selection.

---

### Day 1 – Pre-conditioning & Localizer (fMRI)
- **Localizer Task**  
  Run only in the MRI scanner to identify brain regions responsive to faces, scenes, or other content.
- **Pre-conditioning Task**  
  - Participants learn associations between neutral cues (e.g., image pairs).  
  - Completed over **3–5 runs** (up to 5 max; task may end early if **≥90% accuracy** is achieved).
  - Conducted inside the scanner.

---

### Day 2 – Conditioning, Probe, Devaluation
- **Pre-conditioning Refresher**  
  - A brief refresher session, **1–3 runs** (up to 3 max; may end after 1 run if **≥90% accuracy**).
- **Odor Review**  
  - Quick exposure to odors for reminder purposes (**3 trials**).
- **Odor Prediction Task**  
  - Participants predict which odor will follow a conditioned cue.  
  - **3 runs**, **48 trials per run** (~30 minutes total).  
  - Conducted in the scanner.
- **Probe 1**  
  - Tests responses to **pre-conditioned and conditioned cues** without odor delivery.  
  - **2 runs**, **32 trials per run** (~30 minutes total).
- **Meal / Devaluation Manipulation**  
  - Participants eat a meal involving one of the previously conditioned odors.  
  - **Pre- and Post-meal Odor Rating** to assess devaluation effect (~20 minutes total including meal).
- **Probe 2**  
  - Same as Probe 1 but occurs after devaluation.  
  - **2 runs**, **32 trials per run** (~30 minutes total).
- **Recognition Memory Task**  
  - Participants judge whether they have seen specific cues before.  
  - **1 run**, **48 trials**.
